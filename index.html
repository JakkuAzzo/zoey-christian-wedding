Dress code

Formal & Glamorous. Please avoid beige, black, white and red, including all shades of these colours, as they are reserved for our wedding theme.

Invitations & plus ones

Our wedding is strictly by invitation only. Partners are welcome only if included in your invitation. Please provide the full names of all invited guests when confirming attendance.

↗

There is no dedicated guest parking at Lambeth Town Hall; paid parking is available nearby. Parking options are also available around Bensham Hall. Please check local restrictions and allow extra time for Friday traffic.
