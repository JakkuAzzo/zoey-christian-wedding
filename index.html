Dress code

Formal & glamorous. Please avoid beige, black, white and red, including all shades of these colours, as they are reserved for our wedding theme.

Invitations

Our wedding is by invitation only. Partners are welcome if included in your invitation. Children are welcome; please include their names and ages when you RSVP.

↗

Getting there

There is no dedicated guest parking at Lambeth Town Hall. Paid parking may be available near both venues; please check local restrictions and allow extra time for Friday traffic.

✳

Food & accessibility

Please let us know about any allergies, dietary, mobility or special seating requirements when you RSVP, so we can help make suitable arrangements.

Gifts

Your presence is the greatest gift. Should you wish to give more, a contribution towards our future together would be sincerely appreciated; any gift will be received with love.
