ALL THE LITTLE DETAILS

Good to know.

Dress code

Formal & Glamorous. Please do not wear black. We also ask guests to avoid beige, white and red, including all shades of these colours, as they are reserved for our wedding theme.

Invitations

Our wedding is strictly by invitation only. Partners are welcome only if included in your invitation. Please provide the full names of all invited guests when confirming attendance.

Parking & directions

There is no dedicated guest parking at Lambeth Town Hall; paid parking is available nearby. Parking options are also available around Bensham Hall. Please check local restrictions and allow extra time for Friday traffic.

Children

Children are welcome when included in your invitation. Please include each child’s name and age with your RSVP.

Accessibility & food

Both venues have wheelchair access and accessible toilets. Please tell us about dietary, allergy, mobility, accessibility or seating requirements when submitting your RSVP so we can help make suitable arrangements.

When to reply

Please respond by Friday 8 January 2027. Attendance is by invitation and confirmed RSVP only.

PLAN YOUR JOURNEY

Getting there.

Please check current routes, parking charges and restrictions before travelling, and allow extra time for Friday traffic.

Lambeth Town Hall

Public transport: Brixton Underground station (Victoria line) and Brixton railway station are within walking distance. Plan your journey with TfL.

Parking: There is no dedicated guest car park. Popes Road car park offers paid short-stay parking, with a maximum stay of four hours. Pre-bookable spaces may be available near Effra Road through JustPark.

Please arrive early to be seated before the ceremony.

Bensham Hall

Public transport: Thornton Heath railway station is within walking distance. Bus routes 198, 250 and 450 serve the area; check TfL for the latest routes and stops. Plan your journey.

Parking: Paid options include Garnet Road car park and on-street parking on Fernham Road. Availability, charges and restrictions may change.
